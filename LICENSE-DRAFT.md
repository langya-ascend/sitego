## 1. Scope

These proposed terms cover only original SiteGO material that the copyright holder expressly includes in a release under this license. Third-party code, libraries, assets, and notices remain subject to their own licenses. Afu's worker and other withheld/private components are not included and receive no permission through this document.

## 2. Intended Noncommercial Grant

Subject to the final license, the intended grant permits personal study, noncommercial research, evaluation, copying, modification, and noncommercial redistribution of the covered material. Copies and modifications must retain copyright notices, this license, attribution, and a clear record of modifications. Redistribution must preserve these noncommercial restrictions; it must not purport to grant commercial rights the distributor does not hold.

The final grant must identify the licensor and the covered version before it takes effect. No trademark, branding, provider-account, or third-party-service authorization is implied.

## 3. Prior Written Authorization For Commercial Use

**Any commercial use of the covered SiteGO material or its modifications requires the copyright holder's prior written authorization.** Public visibility, a GitHub fork, a Star, silence, verbal discussions, or an ordinary issue reply does not itself grant commercial permission.

For review, the proposed commercial boundary includes using the software in revenue-generating or business operations, including:

- Selling, licensing, renting, or distributing a paid product containing the software.
- Providing paid API, hosted, SaaS, GEO evaluation, integration, or client services using it.
- Deploying it for a company or other business's internal production operations, even when no separate software fee is charged.
- Including it in a customer delivery, managed service, or system integration.
- Using a free offering to support advertising, customer acquisition, a paid product, or another commercial advantage.

Genuinely isolated evaluation without production operation or customer service is proposed as noncommercial evaluation. The final license must confirm this boundary. Unclear use cases require written clarification before commercial deployment.

## 4. Written Permission And Application

Authorization must come from the rights holder or a verified authorized representative through an expressly designated written channel. A signed agreement or an explicit email from the designated authorization address may serve that purpose when it identifies the authorized party and permitted scope.

An authorization should state the covered project/version, permitted use and deployment, redistribution/service rights if any, term, and fees if any. Permission for one party or project does not automatically extend to affiliates, customers, resellers, or a different service.

The publication must supply the legal rights-holder identity and a verified contact method. No contact address or signed authorization is invented in this draft.

## 5. Third Parties And Contributions

Preserve all third-party license and copyright obligations. These proposed restrictions do not replace or narrow rights separately granted by a third-party license. Do not claim ownership of dependencies or provider logos. Review the actual source and image distribution for compatibility before release.

A contribution does not automatically transfer copyright. Before accepting external code, define contribution terms sufficient for the intended noncommercial and separately authorized commercial distribution; do not imply that a DCO alone assigns copyright or grants unrestricted relicensing rights.

## 6. No Service Or Availability Guarantee

The proposed release is provided as-is, without warranties or guaranteed website availability, to the extent permitted by applicable law. Liability limits and termination/cure provisions require final legal review. This license does not authorize bypassing website verification, violating service terms, or accessing accounts without permission.

## 7. Changes And Publication Gate

Future license changes must be versioned and must not claim to retroactively withdraw rights already granted by an earlier release. The current private repository remains unreleased; this draft does not select a final software license or change its package metadata.

Do not publish source until the final license, bilingual consistency, commercial authorization process, third-party notices, contribution policy, excluded components, and technical acceptance have been reviewed.

Reference: the [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) allows commercial use; a prior-permission commercial restriction requires distinct source-available terminology.
